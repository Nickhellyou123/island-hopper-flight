**Island Hopper** — a golden-hour flight game: fly the island-hopper *Kite* through eight golden rings over a tropical archipelago, then bring her home to runway 09 for a smooth landing. Chase and cockpit cameras, a lit instrument panel, a full HUD (altitude, speed, heading, vertical speed, compass, attitude, throttle), synthesised engine and wind audio, and a 150-second race against the fuel gauge.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, just static files.

## Play

- **Arrow Up / Down** pitch · **Arrow Left / Right** bank and turn
- **W / S** throttle · **V** cockpit / chase camera · **P** pause · **M** mute · **R** restart
- Fly through a golden ring to score (+100, more on later laps). Land gently on the runway for +500 and a full tank. Three lives; don't kiss the hillside or ditch in the lagoon.
